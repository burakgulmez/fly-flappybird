What this is. A hobby experiment. The network's connections come from a real brain wiring map (FlyWire FAFB v783), from neurons flies use to detect approaching objects and trigger a take-off escape. The connection weights are fixed. Only 64 numbers were trained with an evolution strategy (about a minute on a laptop). They map six game readings (height, speed, distance to the next pipe, and gap position) onto the input neurons, and read one flap/glide decision from the output neurons. It is not a fly's mind, and a real fly never sees pipes. In our tests all 5 trained networks passed 100 of 100 pipes in each of 200 unseen games.
